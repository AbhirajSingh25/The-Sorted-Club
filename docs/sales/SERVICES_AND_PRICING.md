## Standard Commercial Terms & Policies

1. **Revisions Policy:** Every standard package includes 2 full revision rounds. Revisions cover copy refinements, imagery swaps, color adjustments, and layout balancing within the agreed scope. Out-of-scope functional changes requested after sign-off are quoted transparently prior to execution.
2. **Client Responsibilities:** Client agrees to supply logos, brand assets, imagery, and necessary business information within 3 business days of kickoff to maintain target delivery schedules.
3. **Payment Terms:** 50% advance deposit to schedule engineering squad; 50% balance upon final staging approval before DNS transfer / live deployment.
4. **Ownership:** 100% intellectual property, domain rights, codebase, and asset ownership transfers entirely to the client upon receipt of final payment.
5. **No Long-Term Lock-In:** Clients own their site outright. No forced recurring monthly builder lock-ins.
