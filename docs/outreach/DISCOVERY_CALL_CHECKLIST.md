# Discovery Call Checklist & Qualification Playbook

**Document Objective:** Standard operating procedure for conducting 15-to-20 minute prospect discovery calls and accurately scoring leads for The Sorted Club.  
**Target Outcome:** Qualify the prospect, confirm requirements, align on budget/timeline, and secure agreement to review an official proposal.

---

## 1. Pre-Call Preparation (5 Minutes)

Before dialing or joining the call:
- [ ] Review prospect's current website or social profile URL.
- [ ] Identify 1 or 2 specific friction points (e.g. slow mobile load speed, no WhatsApp CTA, outdated design).
- [ ] Select 1 relevant Sorted Club demo blueprint (e.g. `Apex Agency`, `Savory Bites`, `Lumina Clinic`, `Clarity Coaching`).
- [ ] Check CRM history, lead source, and previous notes.

---

## 2. 6-Point Lead Qualification Scorecard

Score the prospect on these 6 objective criteria during or immediately after the call (1 Point each = Total Score 0 to 6):

| # | Qualification Criterion | Verification Question / Signal | Point (0 or 1) |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :-: |
| **1** | **Has a Real Business** | Active operations, trading history, existing customers, or viable commercial launch. | `[ 0 / 1 ]` |
| **2** | **Has a Clear Website Need** | Experiencing low conversions, outdated design, missing mobile optimization, or starting a new venture. | `[ 0 / 1 ]` |
| **3** | **Has Budget** | Budget aligns with our standard packages (₹14,999 to ₹59,999+ / $400 to $1,500+). | `[ 0 / 1 ]` |
| **4** | **Has a Timeline** | Target launch within the next 30 days (ASAP to 4 weeks). | `[ 0 / 1 ]` |
| **5** | **Is the Decision-Maker** | Speaking directly with founder, business owner, partner, or authorized marketing lead. | `[ 0 / 1 ]` |
| **6** | **Responds to Communication** | Shows up on time, responds to questions, actively engages. | `[ 0 / 1 ]` |

### Score Interpretation & Action:
- **5 to 6 Points (A-Tier / High Fit):** Issue formal proposal within 24 hours via `/proposal/[token]`. Lock in sprint dates.
- **3 to 4 Points (B-Tier / Moderate Fit):** Share demo blueprint link, clarify budget/decision timeline, schedule follow-up in 3 days.
- **0 to 2 Points (C-Tier / Low Fit):** Direct to self-service templates catalog or free consultation intake brief.

---

## 3. Call Agenda & Script Flow (15–20 Minutes)

### Step 1: Rapport & Context Setting (2 mins)
> *"Thanks for taking the time, [Contact Person]! The goal of our call today is to understand where [Business Name] is heading, see what's holding back your online conversions, and decide if our engineering squad is the right fit. If we're aligned, I'll put together a fixed-scope proposal for you by tomorrow. Sound good?"*

### Step 2: Pain & Requirements Discovery (6 mins)
- [ ] *"What is the main problem with your current website or digital presence right now?"*
- [ ] *"Where are most of your customers finding you today (Instagram, Google Maps, referrals, ads)?"*
- [ ] *"Are you losing leads because the mobile site is clunky or lacks a fast WhatsApp/inquiry option?"*
- [ ] *"Which core pages do you need? (Home, About, Services, Case Studies, Contact, Booking, E-commerce)?"*

### Step 3: Solution & Blueprint Walkthrough (5 mins)
- [ ] Screen-share or send live demo blueprint link (e.g. `https://thesortedclub.com/demo/[template-slug]`).
- [ ] Highlight sub-second speed, 1-click WhatsApp booking, and responsive layout.
- [ ] Explain that we customize all copy, branding, colors, and integrations for their business.

### Step 4: Budget & Commercial Alignment (3 mins)
- [ ] Present the matched package:
  - Starter Website: **₹14,999** (5–7 Days)
  - Business Website: **₹24,999** (7–10 Days)
  - Lead-Gen Funnel: **₹29,999** (6–8 Days)
  - Booking Website: **₹34,999** (8–12 Days)
  - E-Commerce Store: **₹39,999** (10–14 Days)
  - Custom Web App: **From ₹59,999** (2–4 Weeks)
- [ ] Explain standard terms: **50% upfront deposit to schedule sprint, 50% on live sign-off before DNS handover**.
- [ ] Mention inclusions: **2 revision rounds and 30–60 days post-launch warranty**.

### Step 5: Next Steps & Commitment (2 mins)
- [ ] *"I will generate a customized proposal with the exact page breakdown, milestones, and blueprint reference at [Client Email]."*
- [ ] Confirm decision date: *"Can we review it together this [Day of week - e.g. Thursday]?"*

---

## 4. Post-Call Actions in CRM (Immediate)

- [ ] Move Lead stage in CRM to **`DISCOVERY_CALL`** or **`PROPOSAL_SENT`**.
- [ ] Check off the 6 qualification score items in the Lead Drawer.
- [ ] Set `Next Follow-Up Date` (+3 days or agreed date).
- [ ] Generate proposal in `/admin/finance?tab=proposals` and email token link.
